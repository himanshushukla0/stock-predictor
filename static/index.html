Curated shortlist for browsing — not a limit. The search bar also queries Yahoo Finance's full universe (~100,000+ listed securities worldwide), so any listed company can be analyzed by name or ticker whether or not it appears here.
What this does and doesn't do. It does not predict direction — at a one-day horizon that is close to a coin flip, and markets price in public news fast. What it forecasts is range, because volatility genuinely clusters (calm follows calm, turbulence follows turbulence) and that is one of the most reliably measurable effects in finance. Four volatility models compete on every stock — ATR, rolling σ, EWMA and GARCH(1,1) — each calibrated on a training window to aim at the same coverage, then scored out-of-sample by Winkler interval score (band width plus a penalty for misses). Because they're compared at matched coverage, a model cannot win by simply being wider. The winner drives the live band; the whole table is shown so the choice is visible rather than asserted. Sentiment and technicals shift the band's centre only, never its width — tone should not be able to masquerade as certainty. DEMO means live feeds were unreachable and synthetic sample data is shown. Not financial advice.
