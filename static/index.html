How to read this. The predicted band is ±0.6 × ATR (Average True Range, a standard volatility measure) centered on the last close, then nudged by news sentiment and by the technical lean — each nudge capped at a fraction of one ATR so a "signal" can never imply more movement than the stock's own volatility supports. The backtest panel is the honest part: it walks forward through history using only past data at each step, and reports hit rate next to band width — because a wide enough band hits 100% of the time and tells you nothing. It tests the volatility baseline only; no archive of historical sentiment exists here, so it says nothing about whether the news scoring works. Direction at a one-day horizon is close to a coin flip, and markets price in public news very fast. Demo means live feeds were unreachable and synthetic sample data is shown instead.
